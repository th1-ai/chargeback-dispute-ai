Deposit terms accepted at booking: 25% of the stay total is charged at
confirmation and is non-refundable inside 14 days of arrival. Cancellations
outside 14 days are refunded in full. The terms are shown on the checkout
page, restated in the confirmation email, and are accepted electronically
at the time of booking.

Policy version: 2026.1
